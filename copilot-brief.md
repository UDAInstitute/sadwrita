# SADWRITA

A monochrome literary archive and publishing imprint.

Design hierarchy:

1. Metro / Lumia Modernism
2. Literary Editorial Design
3. Dark Academia Atmosphere
4. Occult Symbolism

Primary colors:

- Black #000000
- White #F5F5F5

Theme modes:

- Nocturne (default)
- Archive

Core sections:

- Works
- Books
- Archive
- Reader's Space
- Shop
- Manifesto
- About

The site should feel like a literary operating system.

Typography-first.

Oversized headings.

Minimalist.

Monochrome.

The raven head is the official brand mark.

Never use startup aesthetics.
Never use social media aesthetics.
